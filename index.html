Company Profile

Building Enduring Trust & Architectural Excellence

TEMER Real Estate (TEMER Properties) is a real estate development company based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Founded in 2010 E.C., TEMER focuses on developing and delivering residential and commercial properties in strategic locations across the capital.

With more than 350 apartment units delivered, our reputation is built on architectural integrity, uncompromising construction quality, and transparent homeowner partnerships. Whether you are seeking a primary residence in Addis Ababa or managing foreign currency investments from abroad, TEMER provides seasoned expertise at every phase.
