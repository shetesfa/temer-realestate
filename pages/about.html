About TEMER Real Estate

“Your Trusted Real Estate Partner in Addis Ababa”

Established 2010 E.C.

A Heritage of Trust, Precision, and Delivery

TEMER Real Estate (TEMER Properties) is a real estate development company based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Founded in 2010 EC, TEMER focuses on developing and delivering residential and commercial properties in strategic locations across the capital.

The company profile records that more than 350 apartment units have been delivered to satisfied Ethiopian homeowners, diaspora investors, and commercial enterprises. In a market where trust and completion certainty are paramount, TEMER has established an enviable benchmark of reliability.

Our developments are conceived to withstand the test of time, marrying modern architectural aesthetics with robust structural engineering, seismic resilience, continuous backup utilities, and seamless title deed processing.
