How to Acquire Your TEMER Property from Abroad

A step-by-step roadmap designed for absolute legal compliance and peace of mind.

1

Select & Virtual Tour

Explore our interactive 3D model, review verified floor blueprints, and join our sales advisors on a live WhatsApp video site inspection in Addis Ababa.

2

Sales Contract & Reservation

Receive a formal, lawyer-reviewed purchase agreement stipulating fixed pricing, delivery milestones, and specific floor/unit numbering.

3

Foreign Currency Transfer

Transfer funds through official Ethiopian banking channels (USD, EUR, GBP) compliant with National Bank of Ethiopia regulations, earning authorized foreign currency exemptions.

4

Milestone Progress Updates

Receive dedicated quarterly photo and video documentation as structural construction and interior finishing proceed.

5

Power of Attorney (Wekilna)

If you cannot travel to Addis Ababa, empower a family representative or trusted attorney via your nearest Ethiopian Embassy for seamless key and deed reception.

6

Handover & Rental Management

Receive official yellow/green title certificate (Sened). Optionally enroll in TEMER's leasing management program to immediately secure high expatriate rental income.
